About Me

I like making complex things make sense.

Something I sketched a while back, still my favorite version of me.

Hi, I’m Tal. I’m a product designer who likes turning complex flows into simple experiences that make sense.

For the past 4+ years, I’ve designed SaaS products with complex workflows, multiple stakeholders, and plenty of edge cases. I care about the details, ask a lot of questions, and like working closely with PMs and engineers to understand the problem and find a practical solution.

I like being involved throughout the process, from early ideas and flows to the final product. These days, I also use AI to build working prototypes, test ideas, iterate quickly, and make the handoff to development clearer.

I care about the details, but I always try to keep things clear, practical, and easy to use.

Building working prototypes has become an important part of how I design. It helps me understand whether an idea actually works, find gaps earlier, and have clearer conversations with the team before development begins.
